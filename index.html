Community Guidelines

Texas State Roleplay Rules

All players in the Houston ER:LC server must strictly follow these rules to maintain high quality, immersive roleplay.

1. General Standards

  • Respect all community members and staff inside and outside of RP.
  • No mic spamming, ear-rape, or unauthorized soundboards in voice.
  • Follow staff instructions immediately during mod scenes.

2. Fail RP & FRP

  • FRP: Any crash exceeding 25 MPH requires realistic crash injury RP.
  • Driving hypercars off-road or jumping off extreme cliffs is prohibited.
  • Resetting or leaving the server to avoid arrest (LTaP) results in a ban.

3. RDM & VDM

  • RDM: Attacking players without valid prior RP story/demands is banned.
  • VDM: Intentionally ramming players with vehicles is strictly forbidden.
  • Guns should only be drawn when escalations logically demand it.

4. New Life Rule (NLR)

  • Upon dying, your character forgets all memory of the preceding scene.
  • Returning to your death location within 10 minutes is strictly prohibited.
  • Seeking revenge on the player who killed you breaks NLR rules.

5. Value Your Life (NVL)

  • You must comply when held at gunpoint without an active weapon drawn.
  • Do not attempt to tackle or shoot back when outnumbered 2 to 1.
  • Act as if your life is precious in all dangerous situations.

6. Priorities & Cooldowns

  • Check priority statuses before starting bank or jewelry store robberies.
  • Initiating a major scene during a Priority Cooldown is penalized.
  • Only 1 major priority (e.g. robbery) may occur at any time.
